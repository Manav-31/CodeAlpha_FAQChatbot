Admissions 2027–28

Ashwood College admits students to undergraduate and postgraduate programmes every year. Applications are made online through the admissions portal; paper forms are also available at the admissions office.

B.Tech admission is through JEE Main and MBA admission through CAT, MAT or CMAT. BCA, BBA and B.Sc admissions are based on Class 12 marks. Questions about the process can be put to the admissions help desk using the chat button at the bottom right of this page.

Documents required

  1. Class 10 and Class 12 marksheets
  2. Transfer certificate
  3. Entrance exam scorecard, where applicable
  4. ID proof (Aadhaar or similar)
  5. 4 passport-size photographs
  6. Category certificate, if applying under a reserved category
  7. Income certificate, for need-based scholarships

Originals and one set of self-attested photocopies must be brought for verification.
